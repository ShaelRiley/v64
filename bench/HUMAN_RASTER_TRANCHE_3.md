# Human-content raster tranche 3

## Purpose

Tranche 3 holds the fourteen tranche-2 sources, grids, cadences, durations, temporal-stability settings, and review questions constant while replacing every Hyper Real Candidate-3 lane with Candidate 4. It is the matched decision gate for Candidate 4.

## Required outputs

- Candidate-1 and Candidate-4 midpoint stills.
- Candidate-1 and Candidate-4 decoded motion clips.
- Code-only blind still and motion packages with a separately uploaded key.
- Exact reconstruction and entropy benchmark across all fourteen lanes.
- 0.5-, 1-, and 2-second independent-group sweep.
- Pre-key recognizability, color/grayscale separation, temporal-stability, and subtitle-transcription scores.

## Decision criteria

Candidate 4 may replace Candidate 1 only if it preserves the Hyper Real chromatic improvement without a material monochrome regression, produces no unacceptable temporal regression, and does not create a material compression penalty.

## Outcome

Candidate 4 improved recognizability, chromatic separation, temporal stability, and compressed size, but its monochrome separation scored 3/5 versus Candidate 1's 5/5. It is rejected as the frozen base and retained as the chromatic/temporal benchmark for Candidate-5A/5B.

Measured results are published in `bench/results/human-tranche-3/RESULTS.md` and `summary.json`.
