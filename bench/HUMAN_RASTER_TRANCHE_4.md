# Human-content raster tranche 4

## Purpose

Tranche 4 is a compact, controlled Candidate-5 ablation. It holds the original CC0 depth, black-and-white, and screen-capture sources, 40×11 grid, 12 fps cadence, two-second duration, matcher settings, and review questions constant across four palettes:

- Candidate 1 baseline;
- Hyper Real Candidate 4;
- Candidate 5A: dark teal plus dark, middle, and light neutrals;
- Candidate 5B: dark navy plus dark, middle, and light neutrals.

## Blind protocol

All twelve midpoint stills and motion clips use deterministic anonymous codes. Recognizability, separation, and temporal stability were scored before the concealed key was opened. The scored CSV and JSON preserve the pre-key record.

## Outcome

Candidate 5A retained the strongest depth score but reached only 4/5 monochrome separation. Candidate 5B reached 4.5/5 monochrome separation, produced the smallest selected stream, and recorded the lowest automated flicker proxy, but fell to 4/5 for depth after omitting dark teal.

Candidate 5A is rejected as the frozen base. Candidate 5B advances as the sole finalist but is not frozen. The final palette gate is a constrained single-dark-chroma utility search between the Candidate-5A teal and Candidate-5B navy endpoints while retaining all three neutral rungs.

Measured results are published in `bench/results/human-tranche-4/RESULTS.md` and `summary.json`.
